Mount Hood

Mount Hood is a popular tourist attraction located in the Cascade Range of Oregon, East of Portland. Visitors can find an extensive list of outdoor activities to choose from, from snow sports to indoor events at the Skibowl. The area features breathtaking views year-around, adding to its popularity among Oregonians and tourists alike.

Haystack Rock

Haystack Rock attracts throngs of crowds to Cannon Beach during warm Oregon summers. This massive coastal rock formation rises 235 feet above the surface of the Pacific Ocean. While many visitors travel for Haystack alone, the natural beauty of Oregon's rugged coastline makes it a must-see destination.

Crater Lake

As the only National Park in the state, Crater Lake stands out as one of Oregon's most notable attractions. The lake is best known for its deep blue color and extreme depth, which reaches almost 2000 feet in certain areas. The surrounding forests and cliffs create a stunning landscape and a third must-see landmark.
